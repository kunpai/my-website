# About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at **UCLA**, advised by [Prof. Miryung Kim](http://www.cs.ucla.edu/~miryung/).

## Research Interests

My research focuses on the intersection of Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and Computer Systems:
- **LLM-Based Software Engineering**: Code summarization, model calibration, and code-documentation alignment.
- **C-to-Rust Transpilation**: Agentic performance optimization, benchmarking, and memory safety translation.
- **AI Agent Security**: Adversarial mutation testing, prompt injection vulnerabilities, and multi-agent system safety.
- **Computer Architecture Simulation**: Hardware modeling, cryogenic computing architectures, and gem5 reproducibility.

## Background & Education

Previously, I completed my undergraduate studies at **UC Davis**, where I worked on:
- Superconducting architecture simulation with [Prof. Jason Lowe-Power](https://arch.cs.ucdavis.edu/).
- LLM confidence calibration with [Prof. Prem Devanbu](http://www.cs.ucdavis.edu/~devanbu) and [Dr. Toufique Ahmed](https://toufiqueparag.github.io/toufique.github.io/).
- Automated C-to-Rust migration with [Prof. Tapti Palit](https://taptipalit.github.io).
