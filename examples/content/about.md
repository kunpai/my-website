# About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at **Example University**, advised by [Prof. Ada Advisor](https://example.org/ada-advisor).

## Research Interests

I study how to make machine learning systems keep working when the world changes around them:

- **Robustness under distribution shift**: benchmarks built from real deployment data rather than synthetic corruptions.
- **Fault-tolerant distributed training**: checkpointing and scheduling for preemptible hardware.
- **Testing ML code**: static analysis and repair for bugs that only show up at training time.

## Background

Before my PhD, I studied computer science at **Example College**, where I wrote a senior thesis on gradient compression for data-parallel training.

## Outside the Lab

I run the department's machine learning reading group and spend weekends hiking.
